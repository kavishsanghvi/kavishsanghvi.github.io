    • About Me

    I am a backend-focused Software Engineer with over 3 years of experience building, scaling, and maintaining high-impact software systems across enterprise environments. I specialize in Java and Python, and thrive in full-stack environments with React, Angular, Spring Boot, and Django. I am skilled in cloud platforms like AWS and Azure, fluent in CI/CD practices, and value clean code, performance, and collaboration. I thrive at the intersection of code quality and performance — whether I am architecting microservices, writing testable and maintainable code, or optimizing SQL queries to save precious milliseconds. My career has taken me through some exciting chapters. At Amazon, I helped improve sales by recommending alternate products during out-of-stock scenarios, impacting 2 million+ creators. At TD Bank, I modernized trading analytics systems, migrating legacy applications into scalable, resilient microservices. I have worked extensively with cloud platforms like AWS and Azure, built robust CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub Actions, and improved system reliability through thorough testing and smart automation. I bring a strong foundation in databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB), always striving for clean data access and fast performance. Beyond code, I am a passionate problem solver, Scrum-certified collaborator, and someone who enjoys making technology a bit more human. I hold a Master’s degree in Software Engineering. I am currently based in Mumbai, India — always open to new challenges, projects, or a chat over good idea and better coffee. Let’s build something exceptional.
